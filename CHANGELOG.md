## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-07

First public release.

### Added

- Power, volume in dB (slider, typed value, or Quiet / Low / Medium / Loud presets) and mute
- Inputs, net radio and tuner presets, and internet radio search
- Now playing with artwork and the incoming audio format
- Song history for net radio
- Routines that turn the receiver on, play a preset and set the volume in one click
- A menu bar extra that keeps working with the window closed
- Receiver discovery with SSDP, and `--check` and `--discover` command line options
- Universal binary for Apple silicon and Intel Macs
